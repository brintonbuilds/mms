Advanced Polymers via Macromolecular Engineering Conference (APME) 2027

About APME 2027

We are pleased to announce that the 16th Advanced Polymers via Macromolecular Engineering Conference (APME 2027) will be hosted in the beautiful city of Montreal, Canada, May 10-14, 2027. APME 2027 will be the next in a series of highly successful conferences initiated by Munmaya Mishra and Yusuf Yağci in 1995, with recent conferences held in Catania (2025), Paris (2023), Stellenbosch (2019), Ghent (2017), Yokohama (2015), Durham (2013), Cappadocia (Turkey) (2011), Dresden (2009) and Miami (2007).

The main themes for the conference will be:

  • Advances in macromolecular synthesis
  • Dynamic polymer networks, self-healing and reprocessable polymers
  • Sustainably sourced polymers, degradable polymers, recycling of polymers
  • Bio-related polymers and polymeric biomaterials
  • Polymers for applications in energy, light and electronics
  • High performance polymers
  • Polymers at surfaces and interfaces
  • Self-assembly of polymers
